## How To Read This Tree

- Q0: <span style="color: #1d4ed8">How should the labels be read?</span>
  - A0a: <span style="color: #047857"><code>Q&lt;n&gt;:</code> means a question at decision depth <code>&lt;n&gt;</code>.</span>
  - A0b: <span style="color: #047857"><code>A&lt;n&gt;&lt;letter&gt;:</code> means an answer, option, action, or leaf attached to the nearest <code>Q&lt;n&gt;</code>.</span>
  - A0c: <span style="color: #047857">The number groups each question with its answers, so <code>Q0</code> has <code>A0a</code>, <code>A0b</code>, and <code>A0c</code>, while <code>Q1</code> has <code>A1a</code>, <code>A1b</code>, and <code>A1c</code>.</span>
  - A0d: <span style="color: #047857">Answer letters reset for each question.</span>
  - A0e: <span style="color: #047857">Top-level flow options use <code>Yes</code>, <code>Revisit</code>, and <code>Stop</code> as visible names.</span>
  - A0f: <span style="color: #047857">Parentheses show the detail or hint that used to sit after the direction word.</span>
  - A0g: <span style="color: #047857">Nested choices use their specific option names without direction labels.</span>
  - A0h: <span style="color: #047857">Any question that follows an answer is indented one level deeper under that answer.</span>
  - A0i: <span style="color: #047857">When an answer has consequences, those consequences get their own nested question and deeper answer number.</span>
  - A0j: <span style="color: #047857">After choosing <code>Yes</code> or <code>Revisit</code> at the top-level flow gate, nested route questions do not repeat <code>Stop</code> as an option.</span>
  - A0k: <span style="color: #047857"><code>Stop</code> pauses the workflow. It is not proof that the workflow is complete.</span>
  - A0l: <span style="color: #047857"><code>Done</code> is only valid after a verified clean final state.</span>
  - A0m: <span style="color: #047857"><code>Review First</code> is never terminal. It shows evidence, asks follow-up questions, then returns to the originating decision.</span>
